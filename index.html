How I Spend My Time

What my average school day looks like outside of class.

Before School

This is the view from my bed, which is also usually the last thing I want to leave in the morning. My mornings are normally pretty rushed because I like getting as much sleep as possible. Once I actually get up, I get ready, pack everything I need for school, eat breakfast, and leave. I always try to convince myself that I will wake up earlier the next day, but that usually does not happen.

I spend a lot of time in my room, so I have decorated it with pictures, plants, and other things I like. It is probably my favorite part of my house and where I spend most of my time when I am home.

After School

My after school routine changes almost every day. Some days I can get home pretty early, while other days I have activities and do not get home until much later. When I finally get home, the first thing I normally do is eat something and take a shower. After that, I usually tell myself I am going to start my homework, but I end up spending some time on my phone first.

I also watch a lot of YouTube when I have free time. It is basically my way of taking a break before I have to start doing everything else for the night.

Homework and Studying

Most of my homework and studying gets done at night. I usually have work from multiple classes, so my desk ends up looking something like this by the time I actually start. I normally have my computer, iPad, notebooks, and whatever else I need spread across the desk while I work.

I definitely have a habit of starting later than I should because I always think my homework will take less time than it actually does. Once I start, though, I try to finish everything I need for the next day before I go to sleep. Then I pack everything back up, go to bed, and basically repeat the same routine the next day.
