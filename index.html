How I Spend My Time

What my average school day looks like outside of class.

Before School

My mornings are usually pretty rushed. I get ready, pack everything I need for school, eat breakfast, and leave. I usually try to give myself enough time in the morning, but that does not always happen.

After School

What I do after school depends on the day. Some days I get home pretty early, while other days I have activities and do not get home until later. When I do get home, I usually eat something, shower, and spend some time on my phone or watching YouTube before starting anything else.

I usually watch YouTube when I have free time.

Homework and Studying

Most of my homework gets done at night. I usually have assignments or studying for multiple classes, so this is when I spend the most time at my desk. I try to finish everything I need for the next day before going to sleep.
